THE DESIGN IS RICECAL'S OWN, and it used to be Supabase's design system roles painted in one RiceCal colour. That was a reasonable place to start from and it produced a mail that looked like a developer tool: Supabase's neutrals, its 6px radii, its system font stack, and a green button. Nothing about it said which app it had come from, and the mail is the first thing a new account ever sees of this product — it arrives BEFORE the diary does.
